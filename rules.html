Rules & Prizes

Entry for the season is £50 per player. The full prize pot is paid back out through the competitions below.

Current Prize Pot

Prize Breakdown

Overall Winner

40%
£260

Period Competition

40%

The period prize fund is split across the 10 competition periods.

At the current 13-player entry this is £26 per period.

Highest Scoring Gameweek

10%
£65

Awarded to the player who records the highest individual team score in a single gameweek during the season.

Mystery Captain Prize

10%
£65

Awarded for the highest-scoring captain pick of the season.

  • The player scoring the most FPL points during each period wins that period.
  • A period prize is only awarded after the final gameweek of that period has been completed.
  • If two or more players tie for the highest period score, the period prize is shared equally between the tied winners.

Other Prize Rules

  • The Overall Winner is determined by the final FPL points total at the end of the season.
  • The Highest Scoring Gameweek prize is based on the highest single team score recorded during the season.
  • The Mystery Captain Prize is based on the highest-scoring captain selection.
  • Triple Captain scores do not count towards the Mystery Captain Prize.
